Privacy policy

This policy sets out how "{{APP_NAME}}" (the "application"), a private application run by {{OPERATOR}} (the "operator"), handles Google account data.

Users

The application is used only by {{USERS}}. No other Google account is connected to it.

Data collected

  • The Google account's email address and basic profile (to tell which account it is)
  • Gmail messages, labels, drafts and filters
  • The Google Calendar list, events and free/busy information

Purpose

The data is used only to let the bots the operator runs on their own DeepSeek Harness work with the users' mail and calendars. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train machine learning models.

Storage

  • The tokens used to access Google are stored only on computers the operator manages.
  • The contents of mail and events are handled temporarily on those computers so the bots can process them. Notifications of new mail and calendar changes are kept on those computers for a limited time.
  • To compose the bots' answers, parts of the mail and event contents may be sent to the provider of the language model the operator uses.

Sharing with third parties

Apart from the language model provider above, the data is not shared with or sold to any third party.

Compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Third-party apps & services in your Google Account. Disconnecting an account in the application's settings also deletes the tokens stored for it.
